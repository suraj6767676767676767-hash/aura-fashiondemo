Return & Refund Policy

1. 30-Day Hassle-Free Window: We want you to be absolutely in love with your AURA apparel. If for any reason you are not completely satisfied, you may return any unworn, unwashed items with original tags attached within 30 days of delivery.

2. Free Return Shipping: We provide prepaid return labels for all domestic and international orders. Simply log into your customer care portal or contact support to generate your label.

3. Refund Processing: Once your return is received and inspected at our boutique warehouse, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

4. Exchanges: Need a different size or color? Exchanges are 100% free of shipping charges. Select the 'Exchange' option when initiating your return request.

5. Non-Returnable Items: Custom-tailored bespoke suits and final sale clearance items are exempt from returns unless received damaged or defective.
